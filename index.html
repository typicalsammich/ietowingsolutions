Questions

Before you call.

Fast answers to the most common towing and roadside questions.

What types of towing services do you offer?

We provide emergency towing, roadside assistance, light-, medium- and heavy-duty towing, motorcycle towing, accident recovery, off-road recovery, equipment towing, long-distance transport and specialty towing.

How quickly can you respond to a towing request?

Response times depend on traffic, distance and the type of call. For many local requests, Towing Solutions strives to arrive within about 30 minutes to an hour while prioritizing safety.

Are your towing services available 24/7?

Yes. Towing Solutions is available around the clock, seven days a week, including nights, weekends and holidays.

How much does towing cost?

Pricing depends on location, distance, vehicle size and the service required. Call dispatch for the fastest estimate for your specific situation.

Do you provide roadside assistance services?

Yes. Roadside services include jump starts, tire changes, lockout assistance, fuel delivery and help with common roadside issues.

Do you tow motorcycles?

Yes. Towing Solutions can transport motorcycles using equipment and procedures intended to keep the bike secure during loading and transport.

How do I request a tow?

Call our 24/7 dispatch line at (951) 383-6721 or use the contact form. Share your location, vehicle type and what happened so the correct truck can be dispatched.
